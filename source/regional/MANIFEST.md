## Not found / problems

- **Vol. XIII Coorg**: there is no 1931 volume on Internet Archive. Searches found only the 1881-1921 and 1951 Coorg volumes. Coorg totals appear only in Vol. I Part II Table XV.
- **Sikkim**: there is no separate volume. Sikkim is Part C of Bengal Table XV (leaf 202 of in.ernet.dli.2015.56024).
- **Vol. XVI Delhi** (in.ernet.dli.2015.62805): Table XV intro is at leaf 207 (p. liii), but leaf 208 (p. liv) is blank in the scan. This is the only copy found. Use Vol. I Part II for Delhi totals.
- **Vol. IX Bombay Cities**: there is no city language table. Bombay City and the other cities are rows in Vol. VIII Table XV.
- District breakdown absent: Vol. XXI Cochin and XXVI Ajmer-Merwara (state/province only), and XXVIII Travancore Part I (administrative/natural divisions only). Vol. II Andamans is split only into island groups.
- in.ernet.dli.2015.116063 is titled 'J&K Part II' but actually contains Cochin Vol. XXI.
- These OCR index files returned HTTP 500 during the check, so the items were not analysed: tdl.49856 (Vol. I Part II), in.gov.ignca.31103 (Baluchistan), in.ernet.dli.2015.531275 (Hyderabad).
- Items labelled 'Pt.2 Report' (Bengal 56024, CP 56037, Madras 56039, Punjab 56042, UP 56044) are in fact the Part II Tables volumes.
- DLI `in.ernet.dli.2015.*` and IGNCA `in.gov.ignca.*` copies usually share the same scan and the same leaf count, so leaf numbers carry over between them. Confirm this before relying on it.
- Printed page numbers are taken from the running heads in the OCR and are approximate. The leaf numbers are the authoritative references.
